AI Copilot for Moniepoint

Turning raw transaction data into profit/loss visibility, spend categorization, and anomaly alerts for Nigerian small business owners, recommendation only, by design.

What the survey found

Before scoping the feature, I ran a 17 respondent survey to test both the problem and demand for specific capabilities.

FindingSignal
Profit/loss visibility is the strongest want13 of 17 respondents
Daily/weekly summaries are the second strongest want11 of 17 respondents
Users want to review AI actions before they happen11 of 17 respondents

That last number set the entire scope: if most users want to check the AI's work before anything happens, the MVP cannot take autonomous action. It can only recommend.

What I intend to build

A three stage MVP that would turn a business owner's raw transaction history into plain language financial insight, without ever executing a transaction itself:

  • Profit/loss visibility: automatic categorization of income and spend into a readable summary.
  • Spend categorization: transactions grouped and labeled so patterns are visible without manual bookkeeping.
  • Anomaly alerts: flags on transactions that break a business's normal pattern, surfaced for the owner to review.

The architecture would separate verified transaction data from AI generated interpretation: the database holds the ground truth, and the AI layer explains and summarizes it rather than calculating or altering it. That separation is what would make the recommendation only constraint enforceable rather than just a design intention.

Tools

Google Stitch and ChatGPT supported the design and content drafting process; the OpenAI API powers the insight generation layer against PostgreSQL held transaction data on a React/Next.js and Node.js stack.

What it does

GenZ.ad is an AI powered ad creation platform that lets users generate high quality ad creatives with a single click, simplifying and speeding up ad creation for businesses and marketers who'd otherwise rely on manual design tools.

My contribution

  • Led development of the one click AI ad generation tool, cutting manual design effort by approximately 90%.
  • Wrote PRDs, user flows, and acceptance criteria; coordinated 3 engineers and 1 designer.
  • Shipped a working prototype in 4 weeks and onboarded 10+ beta users within 2 weeks.
  • Ran sprints in Linear and reviewed onboarding and core UX screens in Figma.
  • Triaged and prioritized post launch bugs across the FAQ, external forms, AI creative page, standalone share page, and community filter.

Product analysis: weaknesses and proposed fixes

Beyond the initial build, I ran a product analysis identifying technical, execution, and business weaknesses, and proposed fixes prioritized by team and timeline.

Versioning roadmap

I mapped the product's growth as a version sequence, from an unauthenticated quick start to a full creative and distribution suite:

  • v0.01: Predefined prompts, no login required, for instant first touch ad generation.
  • v0.02: Authenticated image ad generation from campaign, audience, and ad size inputs.
  • v0.03 to 0.04: Editable ad text and image export (PNG/JPEG download).
  • v0.05: Shareable standalone pages, viewable without login.
  • v0.06: Community ad showcase, browsable and filterable by audience and size.
  • v0.07: Direct social sharing to Facebook, Instagram, and X.
  • v0.08 to 0.09: AI inpainting edits and early video ad generation.

Exploring outcome based pricing

I also scoped an outcome based pricing model, charging by ad engagement or time saved versus manual tools like Canva, contingent on integrating ad platform analytics (Meta, Google, Instagram) to reliably measure performance before pricing on it.

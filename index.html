About

Product manager, first-principles operator.

I'm Seyi Ojo, a Product Manager focused on building things people will actually pay for — not just things they say they want. My process starts with primary research, not assumptions: on CareerLink-Up, that meant fielding a 14-question survey before writing a line of the PRD, then cutting the most-requested feature (communities) in favor of the one respondents' behavior actually pointed to (paid 1:1 mentorship), because a cold-start community can't survive a pre-launch, single-founder product.

I work end-to-end: research and market sizing, PRDs and scope cuts, pricing and unit economics, architecture decisions, and — as the sole builder on CareerLink-Up — directing an AI coding agent to implement decisions I made and reviewed, including database-level correctness guarantees for bookings and a real money ledger for payouts. I've also worked on AI-powered product initiatives in fintech, including an AI Copilot built for Moniepoint.

How I work

Research-first, scope-honest, metrics-instrumented.

The same process across every product I've shipped — demonstrated in full in the CareerLink-Up case study.

01

Research before opinions

Primary research fielded before the PRD — small samples reported honestly, as directional counts rather than dressed-up percentages.

02

Scope ruthlessly

Cut the most-wanted feature when the data showed it couldn't work at this stage, and said so in writing rather than shipping everything.

03

Build with rigor

Correctness pushed into the database, not the app layer — money handled as an auditable ledger, not a column.

04

Instrument and report zero

Define the metrics and targets before launch, and report the real number — including when that number is zero.

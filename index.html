How I work

Research first, scope honest, metrics instrumented.

The same process across every product I've shipped: demonstrated in full in the CareerLink-Up case study.

01

Research before opinions

Primary research fielded before the PRD: small samples reported honestly, as directional counts rather than dressed up percentages.

02

Scope ruthlessly

Cut the most wanted feature when the data showed it couldn't work at this stage, and said so in writing rather than shipping everything.

03

Build with rigor

Correctness pushed into the database, not the app layer: money handled as an auditable ledger, not a column.

04

Instrument and report zero

Define the metrics and targets before launch, and report the real number, including when that number is zero.

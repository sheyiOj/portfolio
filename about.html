Hello! I'm Oluwaseyi Ojo, a Product Manager based in Lagos, Nigeria, who turns real world problems into useful, commercially viable products.

My process starts with primary research, not assumptions. On CareerLink-Up, that meant fielding a 14 question survey before writing a line of the PRD, then cutting the most requested feature (communities) in favor of the one respondents' behavior actually pointed to (paid 1:1 mentorship), because a cold start community can't survive a pre launch, single founder product. On the AI Copilot for Moniepoint, a 17 respondent survey showed users wanted to review AI actions before they happened, so I scoped the MVP as recommendation only with no autonomous transactions.

I work end to end: research and market sizing, PRDs and scope cuts, pricing and unit economics, architecture decisions, and, as the sole builder on CareerLink-Up, directing an AI coding agent (Claude) to implement decisions I made and reviewed independently, including database level correctness guarantees for bookings and a real money ledger for payouts.

Earlier, as part of a small team at HNG's GenZ.ad, I contributed the PRDs, user flows, and acceptance criteria that three engineers and a designer built against, helping the team ship a one click AI ad generation prototype in four weeks. At Intern Pulse's PedMonie, working within a cohort team, I contributed the PRD for a unified Nigerian payment gateway and helped plan Paystack/Flutterwave integration and fraud workflows.

B.Sc. Human Kinetics, University of Lagos. Certified in Product Management (HNG, Women Techsters), Digital Skills: Project Management (Google), AI & Product Foundations (Udacity), and Product Discovery (Product School).

The problem

"Users struggle to find convenient, versatile payment platforms, especially in Nigeria." PedMonie set out to solve the problem of managing multiple payment methods for businesses, and eliminating payment friction for their customers, consolidating mobile money, bank transfers, and card payments into a single system.

Target user

Sarah, the Small Business Owner: 32, Lagos, an e commerce store owner with moderate tech savvy. She needs to simplify checkout, offer multiple payment options, keep fees low, and trust that the system is secure. Her fear: losing customers to a complicated checkout, or a security breach that costs her their trust.

My contribution

  • Wrote the PRD and defined the MVP structure for the platform.
  • Planned Paystack and Flutterwave integration for PCI DSS compliant transaction handling.
  • Defined authentication flows, including OTP.
  • Wrote user stories for transaction monitoring, fraud alerts, and security workflows.
  • Ran competitor analysis across Nigerian fintech products to guide feature prioritization.
